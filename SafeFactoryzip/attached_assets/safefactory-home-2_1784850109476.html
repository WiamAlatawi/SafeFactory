The Problem

Maintenance still
happens too late.

Most factories still react to failure instead of anticipating it. That gap is where cost, downtime, and injury risk live.

01

Failures are discovered after they happen

By the time an alarm sounds, the damage is already done — and the fix is always more expensive than the warning would have been.

02

Production lines stop without warning

Unplanned downtime ripples through the entire line, delaying orders and disrupting every process downstream.

03

Repair costs climb

Emergency repairs cost more than scheduled ones — in parts, in overtime, and in the equipment damage that reactive fixes fail to prevent.

04

Workers are exposed to sudden failures

An unpredicted breakdown isn't only a cost problem — it's a safety problem for every operator standing near the machine.

The Solution

One model.
Three questions.

SafeFactory builds a machine learning model that reads sensor data continuously and answers the three questions that matter most on the floor.

Question 01

Will the machine fail?

The model learns the early signatures of failure from historical sensor data — temperature, torque, tool wear, vibration — and flags equipment before it breaks.

Question 02

How severe is the risk?

Not every warning means the same thing. SafeFactory scores each prediction on a severity scale, so teams know what needs attention now versus what can wait.

Question 03

What should the team do?

A prediction alone doesn't stop a breakdown. SafeFactory translates risk scores into a clear, prioritized action for the maintenance team to follow.

How It Works

From raw sensor data
to a clear decision.

A straightforward pipeline turns noisy factory-floor readings into a single, actionable recommendation.

Step 01

Collect

Sensor and process data streams in from equipment across the factory — temperature, pressure, rotation, torque, and wear.

Step 02

Predict

A trained classification model estimates the likelihood of failure for each machine, updated continuously.

Step 03

Score

Predictions are converted into a risk level — Normal, Watch, or Critical — so severity is clear at a glance.

Step 04

Act

The system recommends a concrete next step, routed to the maintenance team before failure becomes downtime.
